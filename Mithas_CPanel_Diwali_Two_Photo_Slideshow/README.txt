MITHAS WEBSITE
================

Files:
- index.html
- assets/ (images)

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Double-click index.html.

How to put it online:
- Buy your domain.
- Upload the contents of this folder to your hosting's public_html / www folder.
- Keep index.html in the root.
- WhatsApp ordering is already connected to 7303345431.

Important:
- The website uses the supplied Mithas design reference and crops its photographic assets.
- Replace the cropped photos with original shop/sweet photos later for the strongest final result.
- Update opening hours and social links in index.html when you have them.
